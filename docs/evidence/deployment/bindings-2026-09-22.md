# Deployment bindings evidence, 2026-09-22

Maps evidence to the `DeploymentBindings` contract (TDD.md, Operations
contracts: Deployment bindings and permissions) and to SDD PL-03, PL-10,
PL-19, PL-22 and SR-28, SR-16. This is evidence toward that record, not a
submitted `DeploymentBindings` row: several of its fields are required and
non-null in the schema (`secret_inventory_ref`, `signing_key_ref`,
`rater_ids`, `operator_id`), and inventing values for them to produce a
complete-looking record would hide exactly the gaps this issue exists to
record. No purchase, provisioning, deployment or credential action was taken
to produce this evidence.

## Application host (PL-03, PL-10, SR-28)

| Field | Status | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `application_host` | unset | #105 is open. Per the owner's 2026-09-22 comment on #76, the host is the smallest rented VM that measured demand supports, sized before #74; nothing is rented before the numbers exist. |

The only measured figures gathered so far are the developer-machine benchmark
recorded as a comment on #105 (2026-09-22): 10 cores, 16 GiB RAM, arm64, CPU
float32, about 2.4 CPU-hours per day at 400 papers, about 5 days of continuous
CPU for a 20,000-paper historical corpus. That machine informs #105's sizing
decision; it is not the application host under PL-03/PL-10. Appendix A: Hosts,
isolation, recovery and spend keeps development, tests, the pilot and the
historical corpus embedding on the developer machine until activation, and
ties the pinned image architecture (x86-64 or arm64) to whichever platform
embeds both the historical batch and the daily flow.

Until #105 resolves and #55, #70 and #83 publish their measured job-record
figures (wall time, peak resident memory, CPU seconds, bytes read/written),
PL-10's stated floor of 16 logical CPU threads, 64 GiB RAM and 1 TiB
persistent SSD with 500 GiB free (Appendix A) remains the specification's
chosen bound, not a provisioned or verified host.

## Private-network reach and rater/operator access (PL-19, PL-22)

| Field | Status | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `private_app_origin` | unset | Depends on `application_host`, above. |
| `rater_ids` | unset | Storage-issued `RecordId`s; no storage service and no provisioned rater identity exist yet. |
| `operator_id` | unset | Same: no provisioned operator identity exists yet. |

The access plan below restates PL-19/PL-22 as the binding target once a host
exists; none of it is realized yet, so there is no HTTPS identity, TLS
certificate or private origin to record:

- The host serves the rating app only over a private network with no route
  from the internet, in either direction.
- Two operator-provisioned rater identities use hashed credentials and
  HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict sessions with CSRF checks and 24-hour expiration;
  there is no open registration.
- The operator role is a separate explicit role, never inherited by an agent,
  and is distinct from the two rater identities.
- The platform enforces this reach from outside the app (PL-19); string
  configuration alone is not evidence of network isolation once a host
  exists.
