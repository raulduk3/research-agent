## Finding: the arXiv-DOI Work undercounts citations

Among the 90 matched Works, 74 have no incoming citations in OpenAlex, 10 have
one, and 5 have five or more. This matches OpenAlex's own `cited_by_count` for
the Works checked, so capture is faithful. The index is what is thin. For
three papers, Semantic Scholar was queried as a diagnostic only, not as a
label source:

| arXiv id | OpenAlex arXiv-DOI Work | Semantic Scholar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2306.05029 | 10 | 26 |
| 2306.15934 | 2 | 22 |
| 2307.00335 | 0 | 13 |

Semantic Scholar rate-limited the other requests, so three papers do not
measure the size of the gap. They do show that the arXiv-DOI Work alone
misses most citations for some papers. OpenAlex often indexes a paper's
published version as a separate Work, and citing works reference that
version. The launch labels would come out mostly false or low, and the
fault would lie with identity linking, not with the papers.

This bears directly on source feasibility (#66). Nearly every matched paper
would have a known label, so the 70-of-100 coverage floor would pass in form.
But the labels would systematically undercount. Before any class-count or
qualification step, target matching needs the SDD's "explicit version
relations": the published-version Works linked to each arXiv family. It also
needs a measured undercount rate against an independent diagnostic.

## Faults found by the run and fixed

- A listing page contained a pre-2007 identifier (`math/0510276`). The parser
  now accepts old-style identifiers; selection skips them, since none can fall
  in a pilot month.
- For PDF-only submissions, arXiv serves the PDF as the paper's "source". The
  document media type now follows content. Command ids include media type and
  kind. A document already retained under the generic type is published under
  that type.
- Storage let a job neither reference its own outputs nor read its own
  checkpoint over HTTP. Migration 0004 records the producing job lease, and a
  job's scope includes its checkpoint and its own productions.

## Resumption observed

- The first run was stopped by hand during the cs.LG listing. The next run
  reclaimed that job on lease epoch 2, continued from its 21st committed
  checkpoint, and committed it.
- The cs.AI listing failed once on the pre-2007 identifier. The operator
  started a fresh attempt, which committed 112 pages.
- A document job interrupted by the media-type fault resumed after its fix
  without repeating its completed source request.
